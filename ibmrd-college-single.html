About Us

Dr. Vithalrao Vikhe Patil Foundation's IBMRD

Pioneering quality education in Management, Business Administration, and Computer Applications in Rural & Semi-Urban Maharashtra.

35+

Years Legacy

Nurturing Professional Excellence & Ethical Values

Institute of Business Management and Rural Development (IBMRD) was established under the visionary leadership of Padma Bhushan Dr. Balasaheb Vikhe Patil to deliver world-class management and technological education to students across Maharashtra.

Our institute is approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, recognized by Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Govt. of Maharashtra, and affiliated with the prestigious Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU).

Our Vision

To emerge as a premier management and computer education institution dedicated to nurturing socially responsible, innovative, and globally competent leaders equipped to meet corporate and community challenges with integrity.

Our Mission

To provide experiential, value-based professional education, foster entrepreneurial mindset through industry collaboration, facilitate cutting-edge digital literacy, and contribute constructively to rural socioeconomic upliftment.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Intake: 180 Seats | SPPU Affiliated

MBA is a rigorous 2-year postgraduate management course focused on strategic leadership, business analytics, managerial decision-making, and organizational leadership across global industries.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters) Full-time with Summer Internship Project

Eligibility

Bachelor's Degree in any stream with minimum 50% marks (45% for reserved category) + MAH-CET / CAT / CMAT.

Specializations

Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management (HR), Operations & Supply Chain, Business Analytics.

Career Prospects

Business Analyst, Marketing Manager, Financial Consultant, HR Specialist, Operations Executive.

DTE Code: 5108 | CAP Rounds Eligible

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

Intake: 60 Seats | SPPU Affiliated

MCA is a 2-year comprehensive postgraduate program designed to transform aspiring techies into proficient software developers, cloud architects, and full-stack software engineers.

Duration

2 Years (4 Semesters) with Full Semester Live Industry Project

Eligibility

Graduation (BCA, BSc IT, BSc CS, BE) with minimum 50% marks & Mathematics in 12th/Graduation + MAH-MCA-CET.

Core Subjects

Cloud Computing, Full Stack Web Development, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Python, Java, DevOps.

Career Prospects

Software Engineer, Data Engineer, Full Stack Developer, Systems Analyst, DevOps Architect.

DTE Code: 5108 | State Merit Based

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

Intake: 80 Seats | SPPU Affiliated

BBA is a 3-year undergraduate foundation program aimed at cultivating entrepreneurial acumen, management basics, corporate communication, and commercial enterprise knowledge.

Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters) Under SPPU Pune Curriculum

Eligibility

12th Pass (HSC) in Arts, Commerce, or Science with minimum qualifying marks + CET as per state norms.

Key Focus

Principles of Management, Business Economics, Accounting, Marketing Management, Entrepreneurship.

Career Prospects

Corporate Executive, Business Development Associate, Sales Manager, Startup Founder.

Affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

Intake: 80 Seats | SPPU Affiliated

BCA is an intensive 3-year undergraduate course providing deep theoretical and practical expertise in software systems, web engineering, database handling, and software architecture.

Duration

3 Years (6 Semesters) Full-time with hands-on lab sessions

Eligibility

12th Pass (HSC) in Science / Commerce with English and Mathematics / Statistics or relevant subject.

Core Subjects

C, C++, Java, Web Technologies (HTML/CSS/JS), Database Management (SQL), Operating Systems, Software Testing.

Career Prospects

Junior Software Developer, Web Designer, IT Support Specialist, Database Administrator, App Developer.

Modern Computer Labs & Certification Support
